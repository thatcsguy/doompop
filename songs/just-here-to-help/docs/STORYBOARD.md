# I'm Just Here to Help: storyboard

Draft for Patrick's approval, one row per lyric line. Time is when the line starts being sung (m:ss.s, from `data/lyrics.json`). `~` marks lines where the aligner was unsure, so those times may move by a beat or so after an ear check. Shots will cut on the bar line nearest each sung start. The song runs 3:46.8, and the beat grid starts at 0:11.3 at about 103 BPM.

Clawd's mood is in brackets.
